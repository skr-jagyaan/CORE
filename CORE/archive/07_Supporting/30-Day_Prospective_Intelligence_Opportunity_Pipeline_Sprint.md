# 30-Day Prospective Intelligence & Opportunity Pipeline Sprint™

## Core Positioning

> **I help advanced-industry leaders identify 3–5 high-value opportunities using Prospective Intelligence.**

The purpose of the 30-day sprint is not to teach AI for its own sake.

It is to help a leader build a **repeatable opportunity discovery and validation system** that continuously turns signals about the future into concrete opportunities that can be tested before significant time, money, and resources are committed.

The AI Personal OS is the enabling mechanism.

The commercial outcome is a **pipeline of opportunities with evidence behind them**.

# The Core Promise

## Find opportunities before you invest.

The client leaves the sprint with a system that helps them:

1. **See** emerging signals and changes that could create strategic opportunities.
2. **Identify** concrete business opportunities from those signals.
3. **Build** a pipeline of opportunities rather than relying on one idea at a time.
4. **Prioritize** the opportunities worth testing.
5. **Test** them cheaply before committing significant resources.

8. **Continue the pipeline** so the process becomes an ongoing source of growth opportunities.

The desired state is:

> **Always have 3–5 credible growth opportunities being investigated, with the strongest ones moving through evidence-based validation before major investment.**

## Day 15 — Identify the Highest-Risk Assumption

## Day 16 — Design the Guerrilla Test

Translate the key assumption into an XYZ hypothesis:

> **At least X% of Y will Z.**

Then define:

- Biggest Unknown
- XYZ Hypothesis
- Smallest Test
- Target Audience
- Standard of Proof
- Test Duration
- Data to Capture
- Stop Conditions

**Guerrilla Test Card**

- Paid Pilot

The objective is to **test the opportunity**.

# WEEK 4 — DECIDE
### Convert Evidence into Investment Choices

The objective is not to validate everything.

The objective is to determine **where to invest next**.

---

## Days 22–23 — Review the Evidence

Compare:

**Hypothesis vs Evidence**

Determine:

- Was the Standard of Proof met?
- What was validated?
- What failed?
- What remains uncertain?
- What changed?
- What deserves another test?

---

# The Decision Fork

## GO

The evidence justifies further investment.

Move toward higher-fidelity validation or development.

---

## MODIFY

The opportunity appears promising, but:

- segment
- offer
- value proposition
- business model
- channel

needs modification.

Return to the Opportunity Pipeline and retest.

---

## STOP

The evidence does not justify further investment.

Retire the opportunity.

Preserve the learning.

Free the resources.

---

## Days 24–27 — Indicative Test

If a Guerrilla Test passes, increase the level of commitment.

Test:

- commercial intent
- willingness to pay
- delivery feasibility
- operational viability

Possible evidence:

- Letter of Intent
- Mock Sale
- Pre-order
- Paid Pilot
- Concierge Delivery
- Commercial Commitment

The progression is:

**Interest → Behavior → Commitment → Investment**

---

## Day 28 — Rebuild the Opportunity Pipeline

The decision on one opportunity should not end the system.

The client returns to the pipeline.

Add:

- new signals
- new opportunities
- modified opportunities
- opportunities released by failed tests
- adjacent opportunities revealed through testing

This keeps the system alive.

---

## Day 29 — Make the Investment Decision

For the tested opportunity:

**GO / MODIFY / STOP**

For the broader portfolio:

- Which opportunities should be tested next?
- Which should be watched?
- Which should be retired?
- Where should resources be concentrated?
