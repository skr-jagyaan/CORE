# CORE Landing Page System
## Version 2.0

### Purpose

This document defines the landing-page architecture for the three commercial transformation states and the Opportunity Signal entry point.

The landing pages should be written from the Offer Architecture and Message House.

They are **not independent positioning exercises**.

---

# 1. Landing Page System

The commercial pages should correspond to:

1. **Opportunity Signal**
2. **₹499 CORE Essentials**
3. **₹39,999 CORE 30-Day Sprint**
4. **₹5L Golden Thread Management System™**

Each page has a different job.

---

# 2. Opportunity Signal Landing Page

## Visitor state

> **Signal**

They have noticed something but may not yet have structured it.

## Primary job

Help the visitor recognize:

> **“There may be an opportunity hiding in this signal.”**

## Narrative

1. Situation
2. Why signals are easy to miss
3. What an opportunity signal looks like
4. Practical tool/resource
5. What the visitor can discover
6. Entry into CORE

## CTA

Experience the Opportunity Signal.

## Success condition

The visitor finishes with:

> **“I have an opportunity I should work through.”**

> **Signal / early idea**

> **Turn a business signal into a structured opportunity and build a credible case for testing it.**

- observations
- assumptions
- ideas
- alternatives
- uncertainty

> **Signal → Canvas → CORE™ GPT → Narrative → Stakeholder → Test**

# 4. ₹39,999 CORE 30-Day Sprint Landing Page

## Visitor state

> **Idea**

They already have a meaningful idea / opportunity they want to take toward market reality.

## Primary promise

> **Bring a meaningful idea into the market, test what must be true, learn from real evidence, and decide what deserves the next commitment.**

This does not mean guaranteed launch, sales or revenue within 30 days.

The Sprint moves the idea through:

> **real-market contact → evidence → decision → next market commitment**

## Problem

The idea remains an internal belief until it meets reality.

Unknowns can exist across:

- customer
- behaviour
- economics
- technical feasibility
- buying
- adoption
- channel
- capability
- ecosystem

The central problem is not lack of analysis.

It is not knowing which condition could materially change the decision.

## Desired outcome

The primary outcome is:

> **One meaningful focal idea moved into real-market testing, evidence, learning and decision.**

The Sprint also installs a repeatable opportunity-discovery pipeline so future opportunities do not have to start from zero.

The existing 3–5 opportunity portfolio remains a **supporting capability** inside the Sprint, rather than the primary commercial promise.

## Mechanism

> **FOCAL IDEA → WWHTBT → CRITICAL/FRAGILE ASSUMPTIONS → CRITICAL BARRIER → FOCUSED TEST → EVIDENCE → DECISION**

The broader repeatability loop is:

> **SEE → FIND → TEST → DECIDE → REPEAT**

## Proof

Use:

- actual cases
- opportunity teardowns
- relevant experience
- real evidence where available

## CTA

> **Apply / Start the 30-Day Sprint**

The CTA and qualification should reflect that this is the flagship individual-level product.

# 5. ₹5L Golden Thread Landing Page

## Visitor state

> **Mature product/business / organisational challenge**

## Primary promise

> **Develop the system/organisation that the product deserves.**

## Problem

The product/business may be capable of winning while the organisation is not designed to support it.

Symptoms can include:

- strategic ambiguity
- fragmented priorities
- inconsistent decisions
- weak accountability
- inadequate capabilities
- poor feedback
- strategy not reaching frontline decisions

## Mechanism

> **Strategic Choice → Policies → Decision Rules → WWHTBT → Metrics → Escalations → Frontline Decisions → Feedback**

## Outcome

> **Strategy becomes executable, measurable and learnable across the organisation.**

## Proof

Use organisation-level examples, Golden Thread demonstrations and relevant management-system evidence.

Do not present this as an expanded version of the ₹39,999 Sprint.

## CTA

> **Start a strategic conversation**

This should be a qualification-based conversion, not a checkout page.

# 6. Shared Landing-Page Structure

Each page should use:

### 1. Recognition

> “This is my situation.”

### 2. Problem

> “This is what is actually difficult.”

### 3. Desired state

> “This is what I want to become true.”

### 4. Mechanism

> “This is how CORE approaches it.”

### 5. Evidence

> “Why should I believe it?”

### 6. Experience / process

> “What happens?”

### 7. Boundaries

> “What does it not promise?”

### 8. CTA

> “What is my appropriate next action?”

---

# 7. Landing Page Message Hierarchy

Do not lead with the full internal framework.

Lead with:

> **Problem → Transformation → Mechanism → Evidence**

The framework should become visible when it helps the visitor understand how the transformation happens.

---

# 8. Landing Page Proof Rule

Use proof to support the promise.

Do not invent:

- results
- customer logos
- conversion rates
- validation statistics
- testimonials
- revenue claims
- success guarantees

If proof does not exist for a claim, either:

- remove the claim, or
- state the limitation honestly.

---

# 9. Cross-Linking

Landing pages can cross-link when useful.

### ₹499

May lead toward:

> deeper validation / 30-Day Sprint

but this is not an automatic upsell.

### ₹39,999

May explain that mature organisational problems move into:

> Golden Thread / ₹5L

but should not imply that every buyer needs it.

### ₹5L

Can reference the broader CORE transformation:

> signal → idea → market → organisation

while focusing on organisational capability.

# 11. Landing Page Quality Standard

Each page must answer:

> **Why does this matter?**

> **Why now?**

> **What is the transformation?**

> **Why does this mechanism make sense?**

> **What remains uncertain?**

> **What evidence supports it?**

> **What does the buyer actually receive?**

> **What does it not promise?**

> **What should the visitor do next?**

These questions are already part of the opportunity-narrative discipline inside CORE Essentials. fileciteturn11file6L975-L1011

# 12. Master Landing-Page Principle

> **The page should make the right buyer recognize their current state, understand the appropriate CORE transformation, believe the mechanism enough to take the next step, and not be pushed into an offer that does not fit their situation.**
