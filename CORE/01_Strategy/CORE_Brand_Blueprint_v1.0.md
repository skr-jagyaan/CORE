# School of Thought

## Building Strategic Systems

Organizations create enduring competitive advantage by building strategic systems that consistently produce superior strategic choices under uncertainty.

This theory is the central proposition of the **Building Strategic Systems** school of thought. It provides an important strategic foundation for the body of work, but individual frameworks may be created to solve different classes of problems without all becoming part of CORE.

---

# Core Beliefs

* Strategy is a system of choices, not a single choice, plan, or position.
* Superior strategic choices are outputs of well-designed strategic systems.
* Enterprise capabilities are how strategic systems become operational.
* Sustainable competitive advantage comes from continuously improving the systems that create strategic choices.
* Leaders should build organizations that repeatedly produce superior strategic choices rather than relying on occasional strategic brilliance.

# Brand Voice

## Overall Brand Voice

Every interaction should reinforce the Building Strategic Systems school of thought.

The overall voice is:

* First-principles thinking over conventional wisdom.
* Calm confidence rather than bold claims.
* Evidence before opinion.
* Strategic rather than tactical.
* Practical rather than academic.
* Long-term over short-term.
* Systems over isolated decisions.
* Curious rather than dogmatic.
* Challenge assumptions respectfully.
* Teach leaders **how to think**, not **what to think**.

The objective is never to provide quick answers.

The objective is to improve the quality of strategic thinking.
