# CORE™ Asset-to-Demand Map v1.0

**Status:** CURRENT / INTEGRATION DOCUMENT  
**Purpose:** Connect CORE intellectual assets to audience states, belief movement, visibility, conversation and appropriate commercial routes.

## 1. Purpose

The Asset Factory answers:

> How do we create useful intellectual assets?

Demand Generation answers:

> How do the right people encounter and respond to those assets?

This document connects the two.

The central principle is:

> **The asset should do a useful job in the buyer's current state, not merely promote the offer we want to sell.**

| State | Situation | Core question | Transformation |

# 4. SIGNAL STATE

### Situation

The person notices market change, customer behaviour, technology change, new application, anomaly, regulatory shift or competitor movement.

### Tension

> “Something is changing. Does it matter to us?”

### Belief shift

From:

> “This is interesting.”

To:

> **“This may contain a strategic signal worth structuring.”**

### Strong assets

Signal analysis, signal teardown, Dynamic Problem Demonstration, opportunity observation, newsletter brief, LinkedIn realization, introductory diagnostic.

### Asset job

Recognition + reframing, with self-diagnosis where useful.

### Conversation trigger

> “Where are you seeing this appear in your business?”

### Natural next step

Opportunity Signal / ₹499.

> **You have a signal. Now structure it into an opportunity.**

# 5. IDEA STATE

### Situation

The person has a meaningful idea or opportunity and needs to determine whether it deserves real market commitment.

### Tension

> “This seems promising. How do we know whether it is worth pursuing?”

### Belief shift

From:

> “We should test the idea.”

To:

> **“We should identify what would have to be true and generate the evidence needed to decide.”**

### Strong assets

Opportunity Teardown, Evidence Selection Demonstration, market-entry case, Decision Autopsy, Dynamic Problem Demonstration, YouTube demonstration, detailed newsletter, diagnostic.

### Asset job

Reframing + demonstration; method belief + self-diagnosis.

### Signature ideas

> **An idea is not a market.**

> **Interest is not evidence.**

> **Don't test the idea. Test what could kill it.**

### Conversation trigger

Explore:

- the decision in front of them;
- what must be true;
- what remains uncertain;
- what evidence already exists.

### Natural next step

> **₹39,999 Idea → Market Sprint**

The offer moves a meaningful idea into market contact, evidence, learning and decision. It does not guarantee commercial success within a fixed period.

# 6. MARKET STATE

### Situation

The company already has a product, customer interest, market exposure, technology or opportunity, but uncertainty remains.

### Typical tensions

Customer interest without commitment; pilots without adoption; technical success without commercial traction; unclear pricing; conflicting feedback; expansion or investment decisions.

### Belief shift

From:

> “We need more activity.”

To:

> **“We need the evidence and decision logic that tells us what activity deserves commitment.”**

### Strong assets

Decision Autopsies, evidence demonstrations, customer adoption cases, technical-to-commercial cases, Executive Briefs, market teardowns, live strategic sessions.

### Conversation trigger

> What decision is stuck? What evidence exists? What is missing? What would change the decision?

### Natural routes

₹39,999 or ₹5L, depending on the maturity and problem.

# 7. ORGANISATION STATE

### Situation

A product/business/growth opportunity exists but organisational execution is unreliable.

### Tension

> “We know what we want to achieve, but the organisation isn't reliably making the decisions required.”

### Belief shift

From:

> “We need better strategy communication.”

To:

> **“The organisation needs a management system that makes the strategic choice executable.”**

### Strong assets

Executive Brief, organisational case, Golden Thread demonstration, Decision Autopsy, leadership session, organisational transformation case.

### Signature ideas

> **A product can be capable of winning while the organisation around it is not designed to make it win.**

### Conversation trigger

Explore strategic choice, policies, decision rules, metrics, escalation, frontline behaviour and feedback.

### Natural next step

> **₹5L Strategic Conversation**

> **You have a product/business. Now build the organisation/system it deserves.**

# 12. Belief-movement rule

Every major asset should answer:

```text
WHAT DO THEY BELIEVE NOW?
↓
WHY IS THAT INCOMPLETE?
↓
WHAT SHOULD THEY BELIEVE INSTEAD?
↓
WHAT EVIDENCE / DEMONSTRATION EARNS THE SHIFT?
↓
WHAT DO THEY NOW WANT TO UNDERSTAND?
```

The next asset should answer that next question.

```text
CUSTOMER SITUATION
→ WORK
→ EVIDENCE
→ DECISION
→ IMPLEMENTATION
→ OUTCOME
→ PROOF
→ CASE
→ CONTENT

```

Proof can become a testimonial, case, Decision Autopsy, Executive Brief or demonstration.

Never fabricate proof.

# 15. Demand quality gate

### Fit

Is this person genuinely relevant?

### Problem

Is there a real situation?

### Importance

Does it matter?

### Evidence

Do we have actual information rather than enthusiasm?

### Decision

Is there a meaningful decision?

### Capability

Is CORE actually positioned to help?

### Readiness

Is there enough willingness to explore?

A like is not qualified demand.

# 16. Metrics

Track separately:

- Visibility
- Recognition
- Engagement
- Conversation
- Qualification
- Commercial movement
- Customer outcome
- Learning

The objective is to improve the movement, not maximise one number.

# 17. Final principle

> **Every important asset should perform a useful job for the audience and create a natural path to the next useful layer.**

The desired movement is:

> **See → Understand → Recognise → Diagnose → Discuss → Decide → Commit**

And for CORE:

> **Visibility → Recognition → Conversation → Demand → Experience → Proof → Stronger Visibility**
