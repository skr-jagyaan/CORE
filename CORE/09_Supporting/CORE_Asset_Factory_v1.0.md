# CORE™ Asset Factory v1.0

**Status:** CURRENT / BUILD DOCUMENT  
**Purpose:** Turn CORE intellectual work into a repeatable portfolio of high-value public, pre-sold, commercial and proof assets.

## 1. Purpose

The Asset Factory answers:

> **How does one important piece of CORE thinking become a family of useful market-facing assets without becoming content production for its own sake?**

It is not a content calendar. It is a production discipline that begins with reality, creates a defensible intellectual idea, expresses that idea in the right forms, and routes people toward the next useful CORE experience.

The governing rule is:

> **One strong intellectual idea should create a family of assets, not eight disconnected pieces.**

## 3. What counts as a CORE asset?

A CORE asset should do at least one of these jobs:

- change how the intended audience sees a meaningful business situation;
- demonstrate how CORE thinks;
- enable self-diagnosis;
- create useful conversation;
- build authority;
- make a CORE method tangible;
- prepare someone for a deeper CORE experience;
- capture or communicate proof.

Before producing, ask:

1. What real observation or problem generated this?
2. What does the audience currently believe?
3. What should they understand differently?
4. What evidence earns that shift?
5. What CORE principle is being demonstrated?
6. What useful next step becomes possible?

## 7. Research and synthesis

Research should challenge, narrow and strengthen the idea. It may include customer conversations, secondary research, technical material, regulatory material, cases, company evidence or market developments.

Do not turn research into a summary. The output should become **CORE interpretation**.

## 11. Pipeline

Every major asset can move through:

> **Idea → Research → Draft → Canonical → Propagated → Discussed → Refined → Authority Asset → Case / Proof**

Not every asset needs to reach every stage.

## 12. Quality gates

Before an asset becomes canonical:

### Reality
Is it grounded in an actual situation?

### Depth
Is there something meaningful to understand?

### Evidence
Is the claim appropriately supported?

### Reframe
Does it change how the audience sees the issue?

### Consequence
Does the insight matter strategically?

### CORE fit
Does it strengthen the worldview?

### Restraint
Are unsupported certainties avoided?

### Reusability
Can it support future assets?

## 14. Free / paid boundary

### Public
> **Capability to see**

### ₹499
> **Structured opportunity thinking**

### ₹39,999
> **Applied evidence + decision capability**

### ₹5L
> **Organisational / system capability**

Free assets can provide insight, reframe, examples, part of the mechanism and enough self-diagnosis to create recognition. Deeper work provides guided application, personalised diagnosis, repeated feedback, full decision process and implementation support.

```text

   ├── Newsletter interpretation
   ├── Podcast reasoning
   ├── Community exercise
   ├── Executive brief
```

Ask:

> **“How many useful expressions did one important idea create?”**

Not:

> “How many pieces did we publish?”

## 16. Content debt rule

Do not create arbitrary recurring formats, filler podcasts, generic tips, repetitive listicles or trend reactions without synthesis.

Every recurring format must create at least one of:

> **authority, recognition, intelligence, conversation, participation or proof.**

## 17. Weekly production rhythm

**Monday — Observe:** review conversations, client work, market signals, technical developments and community questions; choose one valuable observation.

**Tuesday — Research:** challenge the idea.

**Wednesday — Synthesize:** create the canonical thesis and brief.

**Thursday — Produce:** create the primary asset.

**Friday — Propagate:** make the most useful channel-native expressions.

**Continuous — Discuss:** respond, ask, learn and capture new observations.

This is a default rhythm, not an inflexible calendar.

## 18. AI role

AI may assist with research synthesis, pattern extraction, content briefs, alternative framing, derivative generation, channel adaptation, editing, tagging and register maintenance.

AI should not independently decide what CORE believes, whether evidence is sufficient, whether a customer story exists, whether a claim is defensible or which commercial route is appropriate.

> **Automate processing. Preserve judgment.**
