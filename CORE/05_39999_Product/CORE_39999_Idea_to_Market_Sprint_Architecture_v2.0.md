# 1. Purpose

This is the master architecture for the CORE™ ₹39,999 hybrid Idea → Market Sprint.

The product exists to help a founder, leader, or innovation owner take **one meaningful opportunity** from an initial idea/situation toward a real-world market decision.

The Sprint does not promise:

- guaranteed product-market fit
- guaranteed revenue
- a universal validation recipe
- a fixed test type
- a successful commercial outcome

It promises a disciplined process for:

> **framing the right problem, structuring the opportunity, identifying consequential uncertainty, choosing the evidence that deserves to be generated, learning from reality, and making the next strategic decision.**

---

# 2. Product Promise

> **Bring a meaningful idea into the market, test what matters, learn from real evidence, and decide what deserves the next commitment.**

The deeper mechanism is:

> **Diagnose → Structure → De-risk → Select Evidence → Test → Learn → Decide → Market Path**

# 14. The Test Is Not Prescribed

This is a foundational product rule.

CORE does not say:

> “Every customer should run a customer interview.”

or:

> “Every customer should run a landing page.”

or:

> “Every customer should build a prototype.”

Instead:

> **The uncertainty determines the evidence requirement.**

The evidence requirement determines the appropriate method.

The method determines the test.

Therefore:

> **Problem → Decision → Uncertainty → Evidence → Method → Test**

---

# 15. Guerrilla / Indicative / Validity

These terms remain useful, but they are **levels of evidence and commitment**, not universal test types.

## Guerrilla

Fast, inexpensive, directional learning.

Question:

> **Does this deserve further attention?**

## Indicative

Stronger evidence sufficient to increase confidence and justify a larger commitment.

Question:

> **Is this promising enough to move forward?**

## Validity

Evidence appropriate to a consequential decision.

Question:

> **Is the evidence strong enough for the decision we are about to make?**

The method can differ at each level.

# 28. Version Control

### v2.0 changes from the earlier architecture

The major change is the explicit insertion of:

> **Problem Diagnosis**

and:

> **Evidence Selection**

between opportunity framing and testing.

The product no longer prescribes a universal test sequence.

Instead:

> **Test Selection is itself a CORE capability.**

Dynamic Problems, Kolb, Roger Martin, Rita McGrath and Stefan Thomke therefore serve as **distinct intellectual lenses** supporting one decision architecture.

The underlying CORE commercial transformation remains:

> **Idea → Market**

and the product remains a **hybrid, personalized Sprint rather than a conventional course.**
