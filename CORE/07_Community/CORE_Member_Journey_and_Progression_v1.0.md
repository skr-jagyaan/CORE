# CORE™ Member Journey & Progression v1.0

**Status:** CURRENT / CUSTOMER JOURNEY DOCUMENT  
**Parent:** CORE™ Community Architecture v1.0  
**Purpose:** Define how people move through the CORE ecosystem and how Decide With CORE relates to the ₹499, ₹39,999 and ₹5L offers.

## 1. Journey principle

The customer should move according to **state and need**, not according to a compulsory funnel.

The governing principle is:

> **Identify where the customer is, then provide the appropriate next capability.**

## 2. Master journey

```text
PUBLIC PRESENCE
      ↓
RECOGNITION
      ↓
INTEREST
      ↓
₹499
SIGNAL → STRUCTURED IDEA
      ↓
₹7,999
DECIDE WITH CORE
      ↓
PREMIUM CONTENT + PARTICIPATION + DECIDE+
      ↓
₹39,999
IDEA → MARKET
      ↓
EVIDENCE + MARKET PATH
      ↓
₹5L / ADVISORY
PRODUCT / BUSINESS → ORGANISATION
```

## 3. Entry from the public ecosystem

People can discover CORE through:

- LinkedIn
- Decide
- Signals & Strategy
- Ideas for Enduring Advantage
- Strategic Systems Thinking
- Manufacturing Strategy Series
- Confluence of Enterprise Capabilities

The public ecosystem should help people identify their current situation.

## 4. ₹499 state

The person has:

- noticed a signal
- encountered a problem
- seen a possible opportunity
- started forming an idea

Need:

> **structure and clarity**

Outcome:

> **a structured opportunity and test-ready narrative**

## 5. ₹7,999 state

The person wants:

- continued exposure
- deeper learning
- access to people
- ongoing scenarios
- tools and methods
- participation

Need:

> **an environment for continued development and application**

Outcome:

> **greater capability + relationships + access to the wider CORE ecosystem**

## 6. Decide+ state

Inside the community, some members want more structured practice.

Need:

> **a repeatable way to think through situations**

Outcome:

> **Business Thinking Canvas™ capability**

## 7. ₹39,999 state

The member has:

- a meaningful focal idea
- a clear market question
- a commercialization challenge
- assumptions that need evidence

Need:

> **real-market testing and decision-making**

Outcome:

> **evidence + learning + next market commitment**

## 8. ₹5L state

The organisation has:

- an existing product or business
- a material strategic choice
- organisational complexity
- execution or capability problems

Need:

> **organisational and management-system capability**

Outcome:

> **Product / Business → Organisation**

## 9. Direct entry

The journey is not mandatory.

A qualified customer may enter directly at the relevant state.

Examples:

> Signal → ₹499

> Community need → ₹7,999

> Qualified idea → ₹39,999 **only after community membership**

> Mature organisation → ₹5L / advisory

## 10. Progression triggers

Progression should occur when the customer's state changes.

### Trigger to join community

> “I want an ongoing environment, not a one-time product.”

### Trigger to use Decide+

> “I need a structured way to think through this.”

### Trigger to enter Idea → Market

> “I am ready to put this idea into contact with the market.”

### Trigger to enter ₹5L

> “The issue is now organisational, not merely product or idea related.”

## 11. Experience principle

Every stage should make the next stage **discoverable but not forced**.

## 12. Journey success

A successful journey produces:

> **better questions → better thinking → better evidence → better decisions → better action → new learning**

## 13. Final principle

> **CORE should feel like a place people move deeper into because their questions become more consequential—not a funnel pushing everyone to the next product.**
