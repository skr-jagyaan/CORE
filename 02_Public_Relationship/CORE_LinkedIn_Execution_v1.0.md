# CORE™ LinkedIn Execution v1.0

**Status:** CURRENT / CHANNEL EXECUTION DOCUMENT

## 1. Objective

> **Make CORE meaningfully visible to 50–100 high-fit ICPs per day.**

This is a visibility objective, not a promise of individual organic impressions and not a target for 50–100 DMs, connections or scraped leads.

## 2. LinkedIn's role

LinkedIn is CORE's primary discovery, visibility and conversation layer.

```text
CORE → ICP
ideas / cases / worldview / evidence

ICP → CORE
comments / reactions / conversations / problems / signals
```

It is both a distribution channel and an intelligence source.

## 3. Three execution lanes

### Own Content

Publish CORE ideas, cases, distinctions, demonstrations and evidence lessons.

### Borrowed Attention

Participate in relevant conversations on other people's posts by adding something worth reading.

### Relationship Visibility

Maintain repeated human contact through thoughtful comments, useful resources, genuine conversations and useful introductions.

## 4. Daily execution model

### Intelligence

Review priority ICP posts, market signals, technology and regulatory developments, creator conversations and prior conversations. Capture what the market is revealing.

### Content

Select one meaningful CORE idea and define ICP, state, tension, belief, reframe, evidence and natural next step.

### Publish

Publish a channel-native LinkedIn asset.

### Participate

Read and respond to relevant conversations. Prioritise substance over volume.

### Capture

Record meaningful comments, high-fit names, recurring language, problems, opportunities and conversation signals.

## 5. Daily visibility portfolio

## 9. What strong CORE posts do

The desired audience movement is:

> **Surprise → Understanding → Recognition → Self-diagnosis → Desire to go deeper**

Avoid manufactured controversy, clickbait and generic motivational language.

## 10. Comment construction

```text
ACKNOWLEDGE THE IDEA
↓
ADD A NON-OBVIOUS OBSERVATION
↓
INTRODUCE A CORE DISTINCTION
↓
OPEN A USEFUL QUESTION
```

Good comments extend ideas, identify trade-offs, add relevant experience, connect ideas or ask useful questions.

Avoid empty comments such as “Great post!” or “Absolutely agree.”

## 11. Comment-to-conversation protocol

### Level 1 — Light recognition

Reaction or brief acknowledgement.

### Level 2 — Substantive response

Add a useful thought.

### Level 3 — Problem signal

The person describes a real business situation → engage + explore.

### Level 4 — Strong ICP + relevant problem

Prioritise relationship.

### Level 5 — Explicit need

Move to direct conversation.

Progression:

```text
COMMENT
→ SIGNAL
→ CONTEXT
→ CONVERSATION
```

Never:

> comment → pitch

## 13. Weekly relationship rhythm

**Monday:** select 10–15 high-fit people.

**Tuesday:** understand current work, priorities, language, problems and signals.

**Wednesday:** publish an idea connected to an observed tension.

**Thursday:** participate in relevant conversations.

**Friday:** review engagement, conversations, new names and new signals; update the relationship record.

- demonstration;

- commercial bridge.

Use engagement to learn which ideas attract attention, which people respond, which topics create discussion, which distinctions create disagreement and which problems recur.

> **Engagement volume is not business impact.**

## 20. Human vs automation

### Automation

Retrieval, formatting, data capture, aggregation, draft generation and repetitive transformations.

### Human

ICP selection, signal interpretation, strategic meaning, CORE point of view, claim defensibility, publication approval, comments, relationship decisions and commercial routing.

> **Automate processing. Preserve judgment.**

## 21. Publication approval gate

### Intellectual

Is the insight non-obvious? Is the reframe defensible? Is evidence adequate? Is the distinction useful?

### Audience

Is it relevant to the intended ICP and state?

### CORE

Does it strengthen the worldview? Does it sound like CORE rather than generic AI content?

### Commercial

Is the next step natural? Does the CTA fit the state?

### Credibility

Can the claim genuinely be stood behind? Has any experience or proof been invented?

## 23. Daily close

Ask:

- Did the right people plausibly encounter CORE?
- Did we publish something worth remembering?
- Did we make a conversation better?
- What did we learn?
- Which person deserves attention next?
- Did a real commercial need emerge?

Do not manufacture commercial movement.

## 24. Weekly review

Review content response, ICP changes, relationship movement, market signals, qualified conversations, offer routing and new learning.

Key question:

> **What should CORE think about differently next week?**

## 25. Failure modes

Do not allow the execution system to become a content factory, news desk, engagement machine, scraping operation, DM machine, automation theatre or vanity dashboard.
