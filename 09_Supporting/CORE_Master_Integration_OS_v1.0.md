## Status

**Master integration layer.**

This document connects the current CORE operating systems and supporting architectures. It does **not** replace their subject-specific source-of-truth documents and does not silently redefine them.

Where a subject-specific authoritative document exists, that document governs the subject. This document governs **how the systems connect**.

---

# 1. PURPOSE

CORE is not being built as a collection of independent products, content channels, automations, consulting engagements and frameworks.

The purpose of the Master Integration OS is to show how they operate as one system.

The integration problem is:

> **How does CORE move from reality to strategic thinking, from thinking to market evidence, from evidence to decisions, from decisions to organisational capability, and from delivery back into stronger intelligence, proof and demand?**

The master system is recursive.

A useful engagement creates learning. Learning improves the intellectual system. The improved system creates better public work, better diagnosis, better client experience and better future decisions.

# 4. OS 01 — SOURCE OF TRUTH / MASTER ARCHITECTURE

## Governing document

**CORE Source of Truth — Master Document Control Register**

## Job

Control document authority, status and version discipline.

It answers:

- Which document currently governs a subject?
- Which documents are supporting?
- Which versions are superseded?
- Which source materials informed the system?

## Integration rule

The Source of Truth Register controls **document authority**.

It does not itself become the methodology for strategy, content, demand or delivery.

### Existing authority rule

When documents disagree:

```text
CURRENT STRATEGIC / TRANSFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
        ↓
CURRENT OFFER / POSITIONING / MESSAGE ARCHITECTURE
        ↓
CURRENT PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE
        ↓
CURRENT PRODUCT PRODUCTION / LEARNER EXPERIENCE
        ↓
CURRENT OPERATING / SUPPORTING SYSTEMS
        ↓
HISTORICAL / SUPERSEDED DOCUMENTS
        ↓
SOURCE MATERIAL
```

A lower-level document may elaborate a higher-level document.

It should not silently redefine it.

# 5. OS 02 — GOLDEN THREAD / STRATEGIC THINKING

## Governing document

**CORE™ Golden Thread — Strategic Thinking Operating System v1.0**

## Job

Turn reality into strategic choice, evidence, decision and organisational action.

## Core logic

```text
SIGNAL → OPPORTUNITY → PROBLEM → CHOICE → WWHTBT
→ CRITICAL UNCERTAINTY → EVIDENCE → DECISION
→ COMMITMENT → CAPABILITY → ORGANISATIONAL RESPONSE
```

## Key disciplines

### Problem framing

The problem is framed before evidence is generated.

A usable problem frame contains:

```text
CONTEXT
+ STAKEHOLDER
+ OBSERVED SITUATION
+ DESIRED OUTCOME
+ CONSTRAINTS
+ TIME / DYNAMICS
+ DECISION TO BE MADE
```

### Strategic choice

The relevant question is not only what could be done, but what choice should be made and what alternatives are being rejected.

### WWHTBT

For the selected choice, identify the conditions that must hold.

Prioritise conditions that are important to the choice, uncertain, and capable of changing the decision.

### Evidence selection

CORE does not begin by selecting a test.

The sequence is:

```text
DECISION
   ↓
CRITICAL UNCERTAINTY
   ↓
EVIDENCE REQUIRED
   ↓
EVIDENCE METHOD
   ↓
EVIDENCE
   ↓
INTERPRETATION
   ↓
DECISION
```

A test is one possible evidence-generation method.

The commercial architecture should recognise four broad states.

## STATE 1 — SIGNAL

The person notices something that may matter.

Natural CORE route:

**₹499**

## STATE 2 — IDEA

The person has an opportunity or idea that deserves serious examination.

Natural CORE route:

**₹39,999**

## STATE 3 — MARKET / PRODUCT

The idea is in market contact and requires evidence, learning, decision or further investment.

Possible routes:

**₹39,999 / ₹5L** depending on the organisational situation.

## STATE 4 — ORGANISATION

The business needs the organisational system capable of executing and learning from the strategic choice.

Natural CORE route:

**₹5L**

These routes are **not** a forced ladder.

A client may enter at a later state.

# 18. WHAT MUST NOT HAPPEN

The Master Integration OS protects against several forms of system drift.

### 1. No duplicate golden threads

Do not create alternative strategic journeys that compete with:

**SIGNAL → IDEA → MARKET → ORGANISATION**

### 2. No test-first methodology

Do not turn CORE into a fixed test catalogue.

Evidence selection is driven by decision and uncertainty.

### 3. No forced offer ladder

₹499, ₹39,999 and ₹5L are not mandatory stages.

### 4. No content disconnected from reality

Content should originate from observation, research, cases, evidence, decisions or earned synthesis.

### 5. No proof without evidence

Praise is not automatically proof of business impact.

### 6. No automation without strategic purpose

Automation executes a defined process. It does not replace the process definition.

### 7. No downstream document silently rewriting upstream architecture

Source-of-truth hierarchy always applies.

# 19. GOVERNANCE RULES

## Rule 1 — Architecture before execution

Before creating a new channel, product, workflow or automation, identify which OS it belongs to.

## Rule 2 — One owner per concept

A concept should have one governing document.

Other documents may reference or operationalise it.

## Rule 3 — Supporting documents remain supporting

A module, template, dashboard, case library or production document should not become a competing source of truth.

## Rule 4 — New insight enters through learning

Meaningful new evidence, outcomes or strategic discoveries should be captured and reviewed before changing the architecture.

## Rule 5 — Version deliberately

Material changes to strategic architecture, product promise, evidence methodology or organisational model require explicit version changes rather than silent edits.

# 20. CONTROL POINTS

The integrated system has six major control points.

```text
CONTROL 01
Is the signal worth turning into an opportunity?

CONTROL 02
Is the problem framed correctly?

CONTROL 03
What choice are we actually making?

CONTROL 04
What would have to be true, and what evidence is required?

CONTROL 05
What decision does the evidence justify?

CONTROL 06
What capability / organisational response follows?
```

These controls prevent the system from jumping directly from observation to solution, or from solution to investment, without decision logic.
