# CORE™ DELIVERY + PROOF + LEARNING OPERATING SYSTEM
## Version 1.0

## Purpose

This OS converts client and learner work into outcomes, decisions, evidence, proof and new intelligence.

Its purpose is to make CORE compound.

Without this OS, marketing generates attention and delivery generates isolated engagements. With it, every meaningful engagement improves the system.

---

# 2. DELIVERY PRINCIPLE

Every engagement should have four anchors:

```text
STARTING SITUATION
 → TARGET DECISION / OUTCOME
 → EVIDENCE / WORK
 → NEXT COMMITMENT
```

Avoid vague deliverables that cannot be connected to a decision or outcome.

---

# 3. CLIENT INTAKE

Before substantial work begins, capture:

- organisation / individual context;
- current situation;
- strategic importance;
- decision owner;
- decision required;
- known evidence;
- uncertainty;
- constraints;
- expected value of resolving the issue;
- timing;
- commitment required.

This becomes the baseline record.

---

# 4. DELIVERY STATE MODEL

A delivery engagement can be represented as:

```text
DIAGNOSE
 → FRAME
 → CHOOSE
 → GENERATE EVIDENCE
 → INTERPRET
 → DECIDE
 → COMMIT
 → IMPLEMENT
 → REVIEW
```

The exact sequence can change with context, but every engagement should preserve decision logic.

---

# 5. DECISION JOURNAL

Every important engagement should maintain a decision journal containing:

- date;
- decision;
- context;
- options considered;
- assumptions;
- evidence available;
- evidence missing;
- decision made;
- rationale;
- expected result;
- owner;
- review date;
- actual result;
- learning;
- subsequent decision.

The decision journal becomes the archive of strategic learning.

---

# 6. OUTCOME TRACKING

Track outcomes at four levels.

## Level 1 — Work completed

Was the agreed work actually done?

## Level 2 — Decision quality

Did the work improve or accelerate an important decision?

## Level 3 — Market / business movement

Did behaviour, evidence, opportunity quality, commercial traction or strategic clarity change?

## Level 4 — Organisational capability

Did the organisation become better able to repeat the behaviour independently?

The highest-value proof is Level 3 and Level 4 evidence.

---

# 7. EVIDENCE OF VALUE

Value claims must be supported by observable evidence wherever possible.

Possible evidence includes:

- decision made;
- investment avoided;
- opportunity killed early;
- opportunity advanced;
- customer evidence obtained;
- pilot initiated;
- commercial behaviour changed;
- cycle time reduced;
- strategic alignment improved;
- management routine installed;
- capability transferred;
- measurable business result.

Do not convert enthusiasm into an outcome claim.

---

# 8. PROOF ARCHITECTURE

CORE proof has five forms.

## 1. Demonstration

Show the method in action.

## 2. Case

Show what happened in a real context.

## 3. Outcome

Show observable change.

## 4. Testimonial

Capture the client's experienced value in their own words.

## 5. Pattern

Show repeated evidence across multiple engagements.

The strongest proof combines method + context + outcome + credibility.

---

# 9. TESTIMONIAL DESIGN

Capture testimonials around transformation rather than praise.

Recommended prompts:

1. What situation were you dealing with before CORE?
2. What was difficult or unclear?
3. What changed in how you looked at the situation?
4. What did you do differently?
5. What happened as a result?
6. What capability or insight stayed with you?
7. Who would benefit from this kind of work and why?

Never manufacture or materially alter the meaning of client statements.

---

# 11. DECISION AUTOPSY

A decision autopsy is a structured review of an important decision after reality has provided new evidence.

Review:

- what was believed;
- what was uncertain;
- what evidence existed;
- what evidence was missing;
- what decision was made;
- what actually happened;
- where reasoning held;
- where it failed;
- what signal was missed;
- what should be changed in the system.

Purpose:

> turn experience into improved judgment.

# 18. GOVERNANCE

### Privacy
Protect confidential client information.

### Evidence integrity
Do not overstate causality or outcomes.

### Attribution
Distinguish CORE's contribution from external factors.

### Version control
Record changes to methods when learning changes the system.

### Proof quality
Prefer fewer strong proof points to many weak testimonials.
