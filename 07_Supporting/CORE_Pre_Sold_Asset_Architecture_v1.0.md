# CORE™ PRE-SOLD ASSET ARCHITECTURE
## Multi-Channel Intellectual → Trust → Demand System
### Version 1.0

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines how CORE™ should use multiple publishing and relationship channels to create **pre-sold prospects**.

It sits beneath:

- CORE Strategic Narrative
- CORE Transformation Architecture
- CORE Positioning / Message Architecture
- CORE Content Architecture
- CORE Public Intellectual Engine
- CORE Conversation Engine
- CORE GTM Funnel v5

It is an **asset architecture**, not a content calendar.

Its purpose is to answer:

> **What should each channel do in making a prospect progressively more informed, trusting, convinced of the CORE worldview, and ready for the appropriate next step?**

---

# 2. SOURCE PRINCIPLES

The current CORE Content Architecture establishes that public content should create:

> **Recognition → Intellectual Movement → Conversation → Deeper Work**

and that content should be grounded in:

> **Reality → Audience Tension → Intellectual Lens → Synthesis → Narrative → CORE Point of View → Conversation → Relationship → Deeper Work / Appropriate Offer.**

The current GTM architecture establishes a state-based market:

> **SIGNAL → IDEA → MARKET → ORGANISATION**

with corresponding offers:

> **Signal → ₹499**

> **Idea → ₹39,999**

> **Mature Product / Business → ₹5L**

The Public Intellectual Engine establishes:

> **Observe → Research → Synthesize → Narrate → Publish → Discuss → Refine → Connect → Develop Signature Ideas → Build Deeper Assets.**

The Conversation Engine establishes that conversation is simultaneously:

- relationship;
- intelligence;
- opportunity discovery;
- content fuel.

These principles mean the channels should not be treated as separate media accounts.

They form one compounding intellectual system.

---

# 3. PRE-SOLD DEFINITION

For CORE, a prospect is **pre-sold** when the prospect has already experienced enough of the following that the eventual offer feels like a logical next step rather than an unexplained pitch:

### 1. Recognition

> **“This person understands my situation.”**

### 2. Reframing

> **“I may have been looking at the problem incorrectly.”**

### 3. Method belief

> **“There is a better way to work through this.”**

### 4. Authority

> **“This person has a serious body of thought and experience.”**

### 5. Demonstration

> **“I have seen the thinking applied, not merely described.”**

### 6. Self-diagnosis

> **“I can see where my own situation fits.”**

### 7. Trust

> **“I understand how this person thinks and communicates.”**

### 8. Readiness

> **“I know what kind of help I need next.”**

A pre-sold prospect therefore does **not** merely know the product.

They understand:

> **why the problem matters → why their current approach may be insufficient → why the CORE way of thinking is useful → why the next level of engagement fits their current state.**

# 5. THE CHANNEL ARCHITECTURE

The eight channels are assigned different jobs.

| Channel | Primary job | Depth | Relationship | Canonical? | Main audience state |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **1. Free YouTube** | Demonstrate thinking | High | Medium | No | Signal / Idea |
| **2. Free Newsletter** | Nurture + recurring worldview | Medium/High | High | No | Signal / Idea |
| **3. Medium** | Reach + essay discovery | Medium/High | Low/Medium | No | Signal |
| **4. Personal Blog** | Canonical intellectual library | Very High | Medium | **Yes** | All states |
| **5. Paid Newsletter** | Advanced interpretation + practical depth | Very High | Very High | No | Idea / Market |
| **6. Paid Community** | Applied interaction + peer learning + proof | Very High | **Very High** | No | Idea / Market / Organisation |
| **7. Free Substack** | Public subscriber layer + archive/distribution | Medium/High | High | No | Signal / Idea |
| **8. Podcast** | Voice, judgment, trust, long-form conversation | High | High | No | Signal / Idea / Market |

This prevents eight channels from becoming eight versions of the same content.

# 6. CHANNEL 1 — FREE YOUTUBE

## Job

> **Demonstrate the CORE way of thinking.**

YouTube is the strongest visual/public demonstration channel.

The existing YouTube architecture defines its role as:

> **deeper demonstration + intellectual trust.**

It should show:

- real business situations;
- counterintuitive interpretations;
- cases;
- opportunity teardowns;
- decision autopsies;
- evidence/test-selection reasoning;
- manufacturing/deep-tech reality.

It should stop before delivering the full paid operating system.

### Belief

> **“This person can see something in my business that I have not articulated.”**

Then:

> **“I want to see how he would think through my situation.”**

### Best assets

- Opportunity Teardown
- Decision Autopsy
- Dynamic Problem
- What Would Have to Be True?
- Test Selection
- Manufacturing / Deep-Tech Case

### Pre-sale function

> **recognition + demonstration + curiosity.**

### Do not

- reproduce the entire paid course;
- read the workbook;
- give the complete evidence-selection workflow;
- become generic AI education;
- become generic entrepreneurship motivation.

The existing architecture explicitly says:

> **Give away the insight → Demonstrate the thinking → Withhold the complete operating system.**

# 15. CANONICAL VS DERIVATIVE CONTENT

This is essential.

## Canonical

The **personal blog** is the primary long-form intellectual source.

## Derivative

Other channels adapt the idea for their medium.

### Example

Blog:

> 3,500-word rigorous essay.

YouTube:

> 15-minute opportunity teardown.

Podcast:

> 25-minute reasoning episode.

Free newsletter:

> 700-word insight.

Medium:

> adapted essay.

Substack:

> serialized/public version.

Paid newsletter:

> deeper implication + decision memo.

Community:

> live application.

This prevents duplicate work while preserving channel-native content.

# 17. FIVE PRE-SOLD ASSET FAMILIES

## 1. Belief-Breaking

Purpose:

> change the current mental model.

Examples:

- Your biggest opportunity may not be your biggest market.
- Customer enthusiasm is not commercial evidence.
- The first problem statement is often wrong.

## 2. Authority

Purpose:

> prove depth.

Examples:

- guide;
- research brief;
- case library;
- flagship essay;
- proprietary framework;
- podcast series.

## 3. Demonstration

Purpose:

> show the capability in action.

Examples:

- opportunity teardown;
- dynamic problem;
- decision autopsy;
- test-selection demonstration;
- live case.

## 4. Self-Diagnosis

Purpose:

> let the prospect see themselves in the problem.

Examples:

- Opportunity Diagnostic;
- Signal → Idea Diagnostic;
- Critical Uncertainty Map;
- Strategic Choice Review.

## 5. Proof

Purpose:

> demonstrate what happens when the capability is applied.

Examples:

- client cases;
- learner transformation stories;
- before/after artifacts;
- decision stories;
- testimonials;
- recorded case reviews.
