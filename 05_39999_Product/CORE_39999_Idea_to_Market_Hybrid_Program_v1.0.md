# 5. The Product Experience

The program is intentionally hybrid.

The learning flow is:

> **LEARN → ATTEMPT → SUBMIT → REVIEW → SESSION → REVISE → TEST → LEARN**

The learner should **not** arrive at a personalized session as a passive student.

They arrive with:

- a completed task
- a real business question
- an artifact
- evidence
- a decision to make

The live session is therefore where **high-value judgment and personalized thinking** happen.

# 6. The 4–5 Session Architecture

The recommended program uses **five personalized sessions**.

A four-session version can be offered by combining Sessions 4 and 5 when appropriate.

## Session 1 — Frame the Opportunity

### Session purpose

Move from:

> **“I have an idea.”**

to:

> **“I know what opportunity I am actually pursuing.”**

### Before the session

The learner consumes short videos covering:

- What makes an opportunity worth pursuing
- Signal vs idea vs opportunity
- Strategic Gap
- Strategy-In-Use
- Alternatives
- Better Choice
- why premature attachment is dangerous

### Required task

Complete:

#### Opportunity Starting Sheet

- What is the idea?
- What problem does it address?
- Who experiences the problem?
- What changed?
- Why now?
- Why this customer?
- What alternatives exist?
- Why do you currently believe this idea deserves attention?

### What the learner submits

> **Focal Opportunity Draft**

### 1:1 session with Sudharsan

The session is used to:

- challenge the framing
- expose ambiguity
- identify missing alternatives
- sharpen the opportunity
- decide whether the stated problem is the real problem
- establish the focal opportunity for the Sprint

### Session output

> **Focal Opportunity Statement**

---

# 7. Session 2 — What Would Have to Be True?

## Before the session

The learner completes video learning on:

- What Would Have to Be True
- assumptions
- critical assumptions
- fragile assumptions
- critical barriers
- customer behaviour
- technical/economic/operational uncertainty
- why asking “Do you like the idea?” is weak evidence

### Required task

Complete:

#### WWHTBT Map

For the focal opportunity identify:

- what must be true about the customer
- what must be true about the problem
- what must be true about behaviour
- what must be true about buying
- what must be true about economics
- what must be true about technology
- what must be true about integration
- what must be true about capability
- what must be true about the ecosystem

Then rank the assumptions by:

> **Consequence × Uncertainty**

### Required output

> **Critical + Fragile Assumption**

and:

> **Critical Barrier**

### 1:1 session with Sudharsan

The purpose is not to teach the framework again.

It is to:

- challenge the learner's assumptions
- distinguish critical from merely interesting assumptions
- identify the assumption most capable of changing the decision
- define the exact barrier to attack

### Session output

> **Validated Testable Hypothesis**

---

# 8. Session 3 — Design the Market Test

## Before the session

The learner consumes short videos on:

- testing philosophy
- smallest credible test
- observable behaviour
- evidence vs opinion
- Guerrilla Tests
- indicative tests
- skin-in-the-game evidence
- test standards
- stop conditions

### Required task

Build:

## Guerrilla Test Card

### Define

> **Biggest Unknown**

### Define

> **Hypothesis**

### Define

> **Smallest Credible Test**

### Define

> **Target**

### Define

> **Standard of Proof**

### Define

> **Duration**

### Define

> **Data to Capture**

### Define

> **Stop Conditions**

### The learner then prepares to execute the test.

Possible test formats include:

- direct outreach
- landing page
- fake door
- mock sale
- concierge service
- manual facade
- prototype demonstration
- pre-order
- paid pilot
- another test appropriate to the assumption

The correct test depends on what must be learned.

### 1:1 session with Sudharsan

Review:

- whether the test actually attacks the fragile assumption
- whether the test is too expensive
- whether the evidence standard is strong enough
- whether the questions are likely to produce polite answers instead of useful behaviour
- what must be observed
- what would cause a change in decision

### Session output

> **Final Market Test**

# 10. Evidence Capture

Use a structured:

## YODA / Evidence Record

Capture:

### Observation

What actually happened?

### Behaviour

What did the customer/user/buyer actually do?

### Commitment

Did they take any meaningful action?

### Deviation

What happened differently from the hypothesis?

### Interpretation

What might this mean?

### Confidence

What does this strengthen or weaken?

The principle is:

> **Reality generates evidence. AI does not.**

---

# 11. Session 4 — Evidence, Learning & Decision

## Before the session

The learner consumes short videos on:

- evidence
- learning
- confirmation bias
- hypothesis vs evidence
- decision standards
- Go / Modify / Stop
- indicative testing

### Required task

Complete:

## Learning Card

> **We Believed That...**

> **We Observed...**

> **From That We Learned...**

> **Therefore, We Will...**

Then prepare:

## Evidence Review

- What was validated?
- What failed?
- What remains uncertain?
- What changed?
- What surprised us?
- Did the Standard of Proof hold?
- What should happen next?

### 1:1 session with Sudharsan

This is the main **decision session**.

The objective is to prevent the learner from:

- rationalizing weak evidence
- overreacting to one signal
- ignoring inconvenient evidence
- confusing enthusiasm with commitment

The decision fork is:

> **GO**

or

> **MODIFY**

or

> **STOP**

or

> **TEST AGAIN**

### Session output

> **Evidence-Backed Opportunity Decision**

---

# 12. Session 5 — Market Path & Opportunity System

This is the final personalized session.

It can be omitted or merged with Session 4 for a four-session version.

## Before the session

The learner consumes videos on:

- market path
- next 30/60/90-day actions
- continuous opportunity discovery
- Opportunity Pipeline
- decision library
- building a repeatable growth habit

### Required task

Build:

## 30/60/90-Day Market Path

Include:

- next customer actions
- next market actions
- next commercial test
- product/technical actions where relevant
- evidence still required
- commitment required
- owner
- timing
- decision gates

Then refresh the:

## Opportunity Pipeline

Include:

- current focal opportunity
- next opportunity
- backup opportunity
- newly discovered opportunities
- adjacent opportunities revealed during testing
- opportunities parked or killed

### 1:1 session with Sudharsan

The final session is used to:

- review the market path
- challenge the next commitments
- decide what should happen after the Sprint
- convert learning into a repeatable opportunity rhythm

### Session output

> **30/60/90-Day Market Plan + Opportunity Pipeline**
