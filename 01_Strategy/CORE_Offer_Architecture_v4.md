# CORE Offer Architecture
## Version 4.0

### Document Status

This is the offer architecture update following the CORE Transformation Architecture v1.0 and Strategic Narrative v2.0.

The organizing principle is no longer price or a simple product ladder.

It is:

> **SIGNAL → IDEA → MARKET → ORGANISATION**

Each offer solves a different problem and creates a different transformation.

---

# 1. Offer Architecture

| Offer | Starting State | Transformation | Primary Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **₹499 CORE Essentials** | Signal | **Signal → Idea** | Turn a signal into a structured opportunity idea |
| **₹39,999 CORE 30-Day Sprint** | Idea | **Idea → Market** | Bring an idea toward market |
| **₹5L Golden Thread Management System™** | Product / business | **Product → Organisation** | Build the system/organisation the product deserves |

The offers are connected but **not a mandatory sequence**.

---

# 2. ₹499 — CORE Essentials

## Job

> **Turn a signal into an idea worth pursuing.**

## Buyer

- individual leaders
- entrepreneurs
- startup founders
- people working in Manufacturing / Deep Tech / related advanced-industry contexts

## Starting point

The buyer has a:

- business signal
- observation
- change
- emerging need
- customer problem
- technology development
- market development
- anomaly
- possibility

but has not structured what it means.

## Core question

> **“Is there an idea here?”**

## Transformation

> **Signal → interpreted problem → opportunity idea**

## Existing product system

The existing CORE Essentials system uses:

> **SIGNAL → CANVAS → GPT → NARRATIVE → STAKEHOLDER → TEST**

The product uses:

- Business Thinking Canvas™
- CORE™ GPT
- opportunity narrative
- stakeholder communication
- smallest meaningful test

The existing product architecture explicitly defines the boundary as preparing an opportunity for testing rather than promising market success or replacing real market evidence. fileciteturn9file2L260-L326

## Outcome

The buyer should be able to:

- explain the situation
- identify the opportunity
- compare alternatives
- identify what must be true
- distinguish known from unknown
- communicate the opportunity
- seek support for a meaningful next test

## Boundary

₹499 does **not** promise:

- market validation
- commercial success
- investment decision
- organisational transformation

Its job is to improve the opportunity **before deeper validation**.

# 3. ₹39,999 — CORE 30-Day Sprint

## Job

> **Bring an idea to market.**

## Buyer

- individual leaders
- entrepreneurs
- startup founders
- leaders with a meaningful growth / commercialization idea

## Starting point

The buyer already has:

> **an idea**

The challenge is to determine whether and how it can become a market opportunity.

## Core question

> **“Can we bring this idea to market?”**

## Transformation

> **Idea → opportunity hypothesis → assumptions → tests → evidence → market path**

## Existing operating loop

> **SEE → FIND → TEST → DECIDE → REPEAT**

The existing sprint architecture already describes the capability as a repeatable opportunity discovery and validation system rather than a one-off experiment. fileciteturn9file3L418-L493

## Capability

The system is designed to:

1. See emerging signals and changes.
2. Identify concrete opportunities.
3. Build a pipeline.
4. Prioritize opportunities.
5. Test them cheaply.
6. Generate real-market evidence.
7. Decide which deserve further pursuit.
8. Replenish the pipeline.

## Opportunity pipeline

The existing sprint uses:

- Prospective Intelligence
- Opportunity Signal™
- Business Thinking Canvas™
- Opportunity Pipeline
- CORE™ Growth Blueprint
- WWHTBT
- Guerrilla Test Card
- YODA evidence capture
- Indicative Test Card
- Learning Card
- Decision Brief
- Decision Library

These are existing mechanisms in the sprint architecture. fileciteturn9file8L712-L808

## Testing architecture

The testing logic is:

> **What Would Have to Be True**
>
> ↓
>
> **Critical Assumptions**
>
> ↓
>
> **Fragile Assumptions**
>
> ↓
>
> **Critical Barrier**
>
> ↓
>
> **Focused Test**
>
> ↓
>
> **Real-World Evidence**
>
> ↓
>
> **Decision**

A five-day validity test can be used as a focused operating unit inside the larger sprint.

## Decision outcomes

A test can lead to:

- **Proceed**
- **Pivot**
- **Kill**
- **Test Again**

The objective is not certainty.

It is:

> **decision-relevant learning before larger commitment.**

## AI role

AI is an enabling layer for:

- challenge
- assumption generation
- research preparation
- test design
- analysis
- synthesis
- documentation

AI-generated simulation is preparation, not market evidence.

Core principle:

> **AI accelerates the search for evidence. It does not manufacture evidence.**

## Outcome

The buyer should finish with:

- clearer opportunity definition
- explicit assumptions
- critical/fragile assumptions
- focused tests
- real-world evidence
- learning
- a decision
- a market-entry path
- a repeatable opportunity process

## Boundary

The Sprint is not:

> “consulting before the real consulting.”

It is the **flagship individual-level transformation**.

It is a complete system for moving an idea toward market reality.

It is not a mandatory feeder to ₹5L.

# 4. ₹5L — Golden Thread Management System™

## Job

> **Develop the system/organisation that the product deserves.**

## Buyer

Mature organisations / scale-ups in Manufacturing, Deep Tech and related advanced-industry contexts.

The current beachhead is typically:

> **₹10–50 Cr businesses**

but maturity is the primary qualification dimension, not revenue alone.

## Starting point

The organisation has:

- a product
- a business
- meaningful commercial potential
- a strategic choice
- or a growth challenge

The issue is that the organisation may not have the operating system required to make the product/business win.

## Core question

> **“What organisation does this product deserve?”**

## Transformation

> **Product / Strategic Choice → Organisation**

## Golden Thread logic

> **Strategic Choice → Policies → Decision Rules → WWHTBT → Metrics → Escalations → Frontline Decisions → Feedback**

## Organisational problem

Typical symptoms:

- fragmented priorities
- unclear strategic choices
- weak decision rights
- disconnected functions
- poor escalation
- missing or irrelevant metrics
- founder dependence
- capability gaps
- weak management rhythms
- strategy not translating into daily decisions
- weak feedback from frontline reality

## Outcome

The organisation should have a system connecting:

> **Strategy → Decisions → Execution → Evidence → Learning**

The objective is not a strategy document.

It is an organisational capability.

## Boundary

₹5L is **not a larger version of the ₹39,999 Sprint**.

It is a distinct organisational transformation.

It is independently acquirable.

# 6. Commercial Relationship

The ecosystem can be described as:

> **Experience CORE → Install CORE → Institutionalize Strategic Choice**

But this is a **conceptual relationship**, not a mandatory upsell sequence.

The practical entry logic is:

> **Signal → ₹499**

> **Idea → ₹39,999**

> **Mature product/business + organisational challenge → ₹5L**

A successful ₹39,999 client may eventually become a ₹5L organisational client.

But CORE must continue acquiring new qualified ₹5L organisations independently.

---

# 7. Product Portfolio Logic

### ₹499

Creates the **opportunity-intelligence entry point**.

### ₹39,999

Creates **businesses/market opportunities**.

### ₹5L

Creates **organisations capable of making those businesses win**.

This is why the three offers belong in one ecosystem even though their buyers, problems and transformations differ.

---

# 8. Master Offer Statement

> **CORE helps people and organisations turn signals into ideas, ideas into markets, and products into organisations capable of making them win.**

The offers express that transformation at three levels:

> **₹499 — Turn a signal into an idea.**

> **₹39,999 — Bring an idea to market.**

> **₹5L — Develop the system/organisation that the product deserves.**
