# CORE Sales Deck Architecture
## Version 1.0

### Purpose

This document defines the architecture for CORE sales presentations.

The deck should **diagnose and sell a transformation**, not present a catalogue of frameworks.

There should be one master strategic structure, with offer-specific versions.

---

# 1. Sales Deck Principle

The deck should move:

> **Situation → Diagnosis → Implication → Transformation → Mechanism → Proof → Engagement**

It should not begin with:

> “Here are our 15 frameworks.”

---

# 2. Opening

Start with the relevant customer reality.

For mature organisations:

> **The product/business may be capable of winning while the organisation around it is not designed to make it win.**

For the flagship:

> **An idea is not a market.**

For the entry product:

> **A signal is not yet an idea.**

The opening should match the buyer's state.

---

# 3. The Problem

Show the problem before introducing CORE.

Examples:

### Signal

> There are interesting signals, but no clear opportunity.

### Idea

> There is an idea, but critical assumptions remain untested.

### Organisation

> There is a product/business, but strategic choices are not consistently translated into organisational decisions.

---

# 4. The CORE Worldview

Use the common belief:

> **Learn before you commit.**

Then:

> **Increase commitment as evidence increases.**

Do not over-explain.

---

# 5. Transformation

Show:

> **SIGNAL → IDEA → MARKET → ORGANISATION**

Then isolate the buyer's relevant stage.

---

# 6. Flagship Deck — ₹39,999

## Structure

### 1. The situation

The buyer has an idea / meaningful opportunity.

### 2. The problem

The idea contains assumptions that may not survive contact with reality.

### 3. The consequence

Resources can be spent building, selling or scaling before the critical uncertainty is understood.

### 4. The CORE approach

> **What Would Have to Be True?**

→ critical assumptions

→ fragile assumptions

→ critical barrier

→ focused test

→ evidence

→ decision

### 5. The 30-Day System

Use the existing Sprint logic:

> SEE → FIND → TEST → DECIDE → REPEAT

and the existing stages:

- configure context / thinking
- discover signals
- build opportunity pipeline
- prioritize
- test
- capture evidence
- decide
- institutionalize the learning loop

### 6. Deliverables

Only include deliverables that exist in the actual Sprint architecture:

- opportunity pipeline
- Growth Blueprint
- testing assets
- evidence capture
- learning
- decision briefs
- decision library
- relevant AI Personal OS / workflow configuration

### 7. Outcome

> **A clearer market path backed by evidence and a repeatable opportunity process.**

### 8. Why CORE

Four books + relevant experience + applied cases + system.

### 9. Engagement

Scope, timeline, responsibilities, fee.

---

# 7. Mature Organisation Deck — ₹5L

The ₹5L deck should be materially different.

## Structure

### 1. Product/business context

What is the product/business trying to achieve?

### 2. Organisational problem

What prevents the organisation from making the strategic choice executable?

### 3. Diagnosis

Examples:

- unclear choices
- policies do not reflect strategy
- decision rights are unclear
- metrics don't measure strategic logic
- escalation is weak
- frontline decisions drift from leadership intent
- feedback doesn't reach strategy

### 4. Transformation

> **Product → Organisation**

### 5. Golden Thread

> **Strategic Choice → Policies → Decision Rules → WWHTBT → Metrics → Escalations → Frontline Decisions → Feedback**

### 6. Outcome

> **An organisation capable of executing, learning and adapting around a coherent strategic choice.**

### 7. Proof

Use organisation-level evidence.

### 8. Engagement

Scope, outputs, leadership involvement, governance, fee.

---

# 8. ₹499 Deck / Sales Page Logic

₹499 generally does not require a traditional sales deck.

If a deck is used, it should remain concise:

> Signal → Thinking → Opportunity → Narrative → Stakeholder → Test

The buyer should understand that the product prepares an opportunity for deeper testing.

---

# 9. Proof Architecture

Use:

- four books
- relevant cases
- IISc-related work
- manufacturing / Deep-Tech experience
- governance credential
- existing evidence

Never invent case outcomes, testimonials, numerical results or client claims.

---

# 10. Objection Architecture

The deck should anticipate objections such as:

### “We already have strategy consultants.”

Answer:

> CORE is focused on moving strategic possibilities into evidence and decisions, and at organisational scale, translating strategic choices into management systems.

### “We already do market research.”

Answer:

> CORE does not replace research; it starts by identifying which assumptions and barriers actually matter before research becomes expansive.

### “We already use AI.”

Answer:

> AI is an enabling layer. CORE concerns the thinking, testing, evidence and decision system around the opportunity.

### “Why not just build a prototype?”

Answer:

> The appropriate test depends on the assumption. Building the product is not always the best first test.

---

# 11. Closing

The close should restate the relevant transformation.

### ₹499

> **Turn the signal into an idea.**

### ₹39,999

> **Bring the idea to market.**

### ₹5L

> **Build the organisation the product deserves.**

Then define the next step appropriate to the buyer.

---

# 12. Master Deck Principle

> **The buyer should leave understanding the problem they have, the transformation they need, why CORE is a credible mechanism, what evidence supports the approach, and what the next commitment is.**
