The key change is that CORE is **state-based rather than price-funnel based**.

> **SIGNAL → IDEA → MARKET → ORGANISATION**

The ₹5L offer is independently acquirable.

---

# 1. GTM Objective

The GTM system should:

1. Make the CORE worldview visible.
2. Help people recognize their current problem state.
3. Give them a low-friction way to experience CORE.
4. Route them to the appropriate offer.
5. Build relationships rather than force conversion.
6. Create a continuous feedback loop from conversations and customers into public thinking.

The objective is not to push every person through ₹499 → ₹39,999 → ₹5L.

# 13. State-Based Qualification

Qualification should begin with:

> **“Where are you now?”**

not:

> **“Which product do you want?”**

### Signal-state indicators

- something changed
- unclear opportunity
- interesting customer signal
- new technology
- emerging need

### Idea-state indicators

- defined idea
- target customer
- product concept
- commercialization question
- market-entry problem
- assumptions not tested
- a specific focal idea they want to take into the market
- access to, or a realistic path toward, real-world evidence
- a consequential uncertainty that can be tested
- authority or willingness to act on the test result

The key qualification question is:

> **“What idea are you trying to take to market?”**

Then identify:

> **What must be true?**

> **What is most fragile?**

> **What evidence would change the decision?**

### Organisation-state indicators

- existing product/business
- mature company
- strategic choice
- cross-functional execution problem
- organisational capability problem
- decision-system problem

# 20. The Core Commercial Logic

The three offers create different commercial value.

### ₹499

> **Creates an idea from a signal.**

### ₹39,999

> **Creates a market path from an idea.**

### ₹5L

> **Creates the organisation/system required to make the product/business win.**

Therefore:

> **₹499 creates opportunity awareness.**

> **₹39,999 creates market-building capability.**

> **₹5L creates organisational capability.**

# 21. What GTM Should Not Do

Do not:

- force everyone toward ₹39,999
- treat ₹5L as an automatic upsell
- make AI the main acquisition message

- use pressure-based qualification
- turn every conversation into a sales call
- make CapEx the universal pain
