# CORE™ Community Premium Content Architecture v1.0

**Status:** CURRENT / CONTENT PRODUCT DOCUMENT  
**Parent:** CORE™ Community Architecture v1.0  
**Purpose:** Define how each public CORE channel gains a deeper member expression inside Decide With CORE without cannibalising the public ecosystem.

## 1. Core principle

The community is not a paywall placed in front of the public channels.

The rule is:

> **Public = recognition and useful insight.**  
> **Member = depth, context, participation and application.**

Premium content should be more consequential, not merely longer.

## 3. Decide / Decide+

### Public Decide

Helps the reader understand:

> **What is really happening?**

### Member Decide+

Helps the member work through:

> **How should I think through this situation?**

Typical premium forms:

- complete Business Thinking Canvas™ walkthroughs
- guided exercises
- member problem clinics
- evidence review
- worked examples
- decision comparison

## 4. Signals & Strategy — member version

Public content identifies and interprets meaningful signals.

Member content adds:

- deeper context
- second-order implications
- competing interpretations
- possible scenarios
- questions to watch
- affected business models or capabilities
- member discussion prompts

The premium question is:

> **What could this mean for people actually making decisions?**

## 9. Cross-channel premium assets

The strongest member assets may combine multiple channels.

Examples:

### Scenario Pack
Signals + case + scenario + Decide+ worksheet.

### Executive Brief
Signal + evidence + implications + decision questions.

### Decision Autopsy
Case + evidence + what was believed + what happened + lesson.

### Opportunity Clinic
Signal + opportunity frame + member discussion + evidence selection.

## 10. Anti-cannibalisation rule

Do not make the public versions feel incomplete merely to create an upsell.

The member version should provide a different experience:

> **Public → see**  
> **Member → investigate**  
> **Member → participate**  
> **Member → apply**

## 11. Editorial rule

One intellectual core may produce multiple expressions.

> **One idea → public expression → premium expansion → community participation**

Do not copy the same article, video or podcast into multiple locations without adaptation.

## 12. Premium content quality gate

A premium asset should answer at least one of these:

- Does it help a member understand more deeply?
- Does it provide evidence unavailable in the public expression?
- Does it enable participation?
- Does it support application?
- Does it create access to peers or live discussion?
- Does it connect multiple pieces of CORE knowledge?

If the answer is no, it probably belongs in the public ecosystem.
