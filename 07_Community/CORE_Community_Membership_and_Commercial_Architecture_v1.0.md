# CORE™ Community Membership & Commercial Architecture v1.0

**Status:** CURRENT / STRATEGIC COMMERCIAL DOCUMENT  
**Parent:** CORE™ Community Architecture v1.0  
**Purpose:** Define the commercial role of Decide With CORE, the ₹7,999 access model, member eligibility, included value, boundaries and relationship to CORE's other offers.

## 1. Core commercial role

**Decide With CORE** is the owned member environment between the entry-level ₹499 product and the higher-commitment ₹39,999 Idea → Market programme.

The commercial architecture is:

> **₹499 → ₹7,999 → ₹39,999 → ₹5L**

These are different levels of commitment and transformation, not four versions of the same product.

## 2. Community offer

### Product

**Decide With CORE**

### Price

**₹7,999 one-time access**

### Role

Provide entry into the deeper CORE ecosystem: premium content, structured learning, scenarios, live interaction, member participation, tools and the member network.

## 3. Customer state

The ₹7,999 offer is for a person who has moved beyond a one-off interest and wants an ongoing environment in which to:

- explore possibilities
- follow important signals
- work through real situations
- learn from other members
- use CORE methods and tools
- participate in scenarios and discussions
- discover the next relevant CORE capability

The customer does not need to have a fully formed business idea.

## 4. What ₹7,999 buys

Membership should provide a coherent environment rather than a bundle of disconnected benefits.

### Core member value

> **Access + knowledge + participation + relationships + application**

### Included layers

- Premium versions of the public CORE publications
- Decide+ structured thinking environment
- Scenario-based participation
- Live community sessions
- Member discussions and peer exchange
- Selected tools, templates and working materials
- Curated archives and case material
- Member network and relevant introductions where appropriate

## 5. Decide+ inside the community

**Decide+ is a premium capability inside Decide With CORE.**

It is not the definition of the community.

Its role is to provide structured application of the **Business Thinking Canvas™** and related CORE thinking methods.

The distinction is:

> **Community = the environment**  
> **Decide+ = one of the capability layers inside it**

## 6. Relationship to ₹499

### ₹499

**CORE Essentials — Business Thinking Canvas™ System**

Purpose:

> Turn a signal into a structured opportunity and a test-ready case for thinking.

The ₹499 product should remain independently valuable.

A ₹499 customer may later enter Decide With CORE, but the ₹499 purchase must not feel artificially incomplete merely to force the community sale.

## 7. Relationship to ₹39,999

### ₹39,999

**Idea → Market**

Purpose:

> Move a meaningful idea into real-market testing, evidence, learning and a next market commitment.

### Access rule

The ₹39,999 programme is **available only to Decide With CORE members**.

This creates a deliberate progression:

> **Explore → Participate → Develop capability → Commit to market application**

The community should not be framed merely as a payment gate for the ₹39,999 programme. It must have independent ongoing value.

## 8. Relationship to ₹5L

### ₹5L

**Product / Business → Organisation**

Purpose:

> Build the organisational and management system required to make the product/business win.

₹5L remains independently acquirable by qualified organisations. Community membership is not a mandatory prerequisite for enterprise advisory.

## 9. Commercial progression

The intended progression is:

```text
₹499
Signal → Structured Idea
        ↓
₹7,999
CORE Access / Community
        ↓
₹39,999
Idea → Market
        ↓
₹5L
Product / Business → Organisation
```

This is a **possible progression**, not a mandatory funnel.

## 10. Member value principle

The community must not depend on continuously adding volume of content.

Value should compound through:

> **better content + better tools + better people + better conversations + accumulated shared memory**

## 11. Commercial boundaries

Do not:

- make ₹39,999 available only as a disguised upsell without community value
- promise outcomes or revenue
- make membership dependent on attendance
- turn every member interaction into a sales interaction
- create artificial scarcity around routine content
- treat the community as a generic networking group

## 12. Access policy

The final duration model must be specified at implementation level.

Preferred commercial default for launch:

> **₹7,999 one-time access**

The platform may implement the access period as defined by the approved product specification. Any future recurring or tiered model requires a separate commercial decision.

## 13. Success measures

Primary indicators:

- quality of members admitted
- meaningful member participation
- member-to-member value
- use of CORE tools and methods
- scenario participation
- progression into relevant CORE products
- useful introductions and collaborations
- retention of active participation

Avoid optimizing only for:

- member count
- login count
- content consumption
- vanity engagement

## 14. Commercial principle

> **Sell access to a valuable environment, not access to a pile of content.**
